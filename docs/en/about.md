# About the site

## Why“TravelDaily”

  To be honest,it's just for fun at beginning。

  I always maintain the habit of keeping travel journals。Things come different since I wrote an English journal titled“[A Wild Ride Along River](en/posts/2026/04/02-AWildRideAlongRiver)”after a ride.First,I polished it with Ai, and wrapped it in a stylish journal template using rich text. I then posted it on our website,  [TravelHub](fbll.asia)。 I even provided word annotations for it myself, just like what others do for real foreign journals. After everything was done, I was satisfied with my "journal". A peculiar sense hit me as I carefully translated my mundane travel journal into English，“bringing an unfamiliar freshness”————just as if what I written in this log，I felt a sudden thrill。English started to charm me by its express,which its really cool

  So here's the website。It offers a fresh perspective on mundane daily accounts, enabling us to rediscover the beauty and novelty of life through unfamiliar expressions.‌Straightforwardly‌,it's for showing off;But in a ‌tactful‌ word ，it's for mutual appreciation of the beauty of Chinese and English languages, learning about the similarities and differences in language expression, and simultaneously enhancing one's own proficiency。Be a genuine foreign magazine website, amplifying the original wild imagination.Meanwhile,I hope more people can share it.To make our stories more listened,and also listen more stories of others....

  Here, you can appreciate authentic English writing, enjoy interesting stories, and share travel essays...Each article includes translations and annotations provided by AI assistants or the authors themselves. You can switch languages at any time by turning on the switch located in the top right corner of the page.You can also visit our website [TravelHub](https://fbll.asia/) for a pure Chinese words if you have interest.


  We strive to make it as easy and casual as possible for everyone to post content.But all the content must be reviewed by Ai or manual admin before they are posted.If something there give you uncomfortable feeling,welcome to provide feedback.We are not trying to preach or instill something；It's for fun after all.Come on,Don't be serious.
